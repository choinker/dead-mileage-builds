About this game

Dead Mileage is a three-player co-op survival game about one vehicle and the road it can't leave. One player drives, one mans the rear turret and one rides co-pilot. You need all three, and swapping seats at speed means climbing over each other in a moving buggy.

Momentum is survival

A horde director watches your speedometer. Under 10 km/h the dead spawn twice as fast and close in from every side. Above 60 km/h they fall behind and chase your dust. Every stop is a gamble you choose to take.

Fuel forces your hand

The rig drinks fuel, and boosting burns it two and a half times faster while heating the engine. Four depots sit around the village and each one runs dry. Pull onto a pad, hold to refuel, and watch the runners close in while the gauge climbs. Ammo caches work the same way.

The night is coming

Each round is one compressed day. Scavenge in daylight while the horde is thin. Dusk brings runners and brutes. Night brings fog, headlights only and up to 250 of the dead at once. Runners cling to the hull below 35 km/h, so drift, jump or floor it to shake them off.

Stop for the settlement

Optional objectives pay out scrap for the riskiest thing you can do, which is stopping. Salvage the wreck for 30 scrap, rescue the survivor for 50, or clear the road to the depot for 25. Kills pay too, from 1 scrap for a walker up to 10 for a brute.

The garage

Make it to dawn and the crew pulls into camp around a burn barrel. Spend scrap across five upgrade tracks before the next day: Engine for top speed, Fuel tank, Turret rounds for damage, Ammo racks for reserve and Armor plating for hull. Each track has three levels, and the rig carries everything into the next settlement.

Features

  • Three seats, three jobs: Driver, Gunner and Co-pilot, each with its own camera and HUD.
  • Solo play with an autopilot that laps the village while you're out of the driver's seat, and a roof auto-turret.
  • Online co-op for up to three players, with the host running the rig and the horde.
  • Walkers, runners that grab onto the hull, and brutes that can stop the rig cold.
  • Bouncy, forgiving arcade handling built for flinging a buggy over dunes.
  • Keyboard and mouse or gamepad.
