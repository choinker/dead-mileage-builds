Drive an armored rig through a desert village overrun by the dead. Speed keeps you alive. Fuel forces you to stop. Survive one compressed day and night until dawn.

Field notes

  • An auto-turret on the roof shoots whatever gets close. It can't keep up with runners clinging to the hull.
  • Runners cling below 35 km/h. Get above 45 km/h, drift, or jump to shake them off.
  • Four fuel depots. Each one runs dry, so plan your route.
  • Night brings fog, headlights only, and up to 250 zombies.
